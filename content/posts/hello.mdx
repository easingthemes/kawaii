This is the first post on the site. I am going to write about the things I go to —
concerts, school events, days out — and put the photos here too.

## How this works

Every post has a title, a short summary and the text. The summary is what shows up
in the list of posts and when a link is shared, so it is worth writing one.

You can add photos inside a post, and there is a separate **Gallery** page for the
ones that do not belong to any single post.
